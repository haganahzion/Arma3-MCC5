# MCC 5 Community Rules

These are the rules for the MCC 5 Discord, our servers and MCC 5 support. The code itself is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 (see [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)). These rules don't change what the license allows, but if you want to be part of the MCC 5 community and get support from us, we ask you to follow them.

**1. No financial gain**
Don't sell MCC 5, charge for access to it, put it behind a paywall, Patreon tier, donation reward or subscription, or use it to make money.

**2. Ask before reuploading**
Please don't reupload or mirror MCC 5 (files, scripts or artwork) on the Steam Workshop, other Discords, file hosts or websites without asking the MCC 5 team first. Point people to the official Workshop page instead: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3840123393

**3. Don't claim it as your own**
Don't rebrand, repackage or present MCC 5 or its code as your own work.

**4. Talk to us before publishing your edits**
Change MCC 5 however you like for your own games or your unit's games. If you want to publish a changed version, talk to us first, and keep it under the GPL-3.0 with the credits in place.

**5. Monetized servers**
Don't run MCC 5 on servers that charge players, sell perks or ranks, or otherwise monetize gameplay, unless you have written permission from z1on_8200.

**6. Credit**
MCC 5 is built on MCC Sandbox by **Shay_gman & Spirit**. Their credit must stay in place.

Breaking these rules can get you removed from our Discord and servers, and you lose MCC 5 support.

Questions or permission requests: contact z1on_8200 on [Discord](https://discord.gg/bxwX5EkMEf).
